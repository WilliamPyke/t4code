# Start here

Tinkjam is a shared board where your team and your agents work side by side. You need nothing
installed to use a board: open [app.tinkjam.com](https://app.tinkjam.com) in a browser.

Tinkjam used to live at app.trellium.org, and it still works there: links to it keep opening.
Boards you joined without signing in stay in the browser at the address where you joined them, so
open those at app.trellium.org. Boards on your account show at both.

## Boards

The home page lists your boards. **New board** starts an empty one, or pick a template such as a
retro or a feature plan. Boards live in the cloud, so they open and can be shared while every
computer is off.

On a board, add notes, tasks, sections and lines, vote, and comment. Paste or drop a link and it
becomes a card: Figma files, Google Docs, Sheets and Slides, and videos show live. Press
**Ctrl+K** (**Cmd+K** on a Mac) for the command menu, which lists everything you can do on the
board and its keyboard shortcuts.

Sign in to see your boards on every device. Without an account, your boards stay in this browser.

## The Plan

Every board has a Plan: its tasks as columns for To do, In progress and Done, or as a list. Ticking
a task on the board and moving it in the Plan are the same change. Tasks can have a priority, a due
date and someone they are assigned to.

## Sharing

Choose **Share** on a board. It has two links:

- **Can edit**: people with it can change the board.
- **Can view**: people with it see the board live but cannot change it.

The person who made the board can move anyone between the two, remove someone, or delete the
board. To share every board with your team at once, make a **workspace** from the home page and
invite people to it.

## Agents

Agents such as Claude Code or Codex run on your own computer, under your own account. To use yours
on a board, connect your computer once with **Tinkjam Sync**:

1. Choose **Connect a computer** in the sidebar, in **Settings**, or in the command menu.
2. On Windows, download Tinkjam Sync there and open it. On a Mac, open Terminal, paste the line it
   shows and press Return; it asks for no password. On Linux, paste the command it shows into a
   terminal (it needs Node.js 22.16 or newer).
3. Your browser opens by itself: sign in and press **Connect**. Tinkjam Sync then sits in the menu
   bar or system tray and starts with your computer.

Then choose **Bring my agent** on a board. Your agent gets its own box: ask it about cards you
selected, have it write a document, run tasks, or plan with you. Others on the board see what it
makes, but only you can start it. Nothing anyone else writes reaches your agent unless you press
**Send**.

**Bring in with my agent** on a link card has your agent read the page and leave a copy on the
board that everyone can use.

## Simple and Advanced

Tinkjam opens in **Simple** mode on the web: boards, the Plan and your agents' boxes. **Advanced**
mode adds the developer tools underneath: chats with your agents outside a board, projects, code
changes, terminals, pull requests, usage and the full settings. Nothing is removed in Simple; it
is only out of sight.

Switch with **Switch to Advanced** in your account menu or the command menu, or turn on
**Advanced mode** in **Settings**. The choice is kept in this browser. The desktop app and a
Tinkjam you run on your own computer open in Advanced.

## Settings

In Simple mode, **Settings** has your profile, light or dark, the theme, your computers, and
**Advanced settings** for everything else. **Remove** next to a computer takes it off your
account and out of this browser, even when it is off; connect it again with Tinkjam Sync to
use its agent later.
