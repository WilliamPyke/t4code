# Interview synthesis: round 1

Eight interviews, 30 minutes each: 3 product managers, 3 designers, 2 engineers. All had made a board in the last two weeks.

## Three themes

**1. "I didn't know where to start."**
Six of eight said an empty board felt like homework. Four asked to see a board another team had made.

> "I'd rather copy someone's good board than design one from nothing." (PM, fintech)

**2. The agent step felt mandatory.**
Five of eight stopped at "Bring your agent". Three thought the product would not work without it.

> "I thought I had to install something before I could even add a sticky note." (Designer, agency)

**3. Links are how work arrives.**
Designers paste Figma links, PMs paste issues, engineers paste pull requests. Seven of eight pasted a link in their first ten minutes.

## What we'll do about it

| Theme       | Change                               | Task on the board                    |
| ----------- | ------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------ |
| Empty board | Templates and a public sample board  | Starter templates, Ship sample board |
| Agent step  | Make it optional and skippable       | Make "Bring your agent" skippable    |
| Links       | Keep paste-to-embed front and centre | Welcome copy for the first board     |

Round 2 starts once six more PMs are recruited.
