# Onboarding v2

**Owner:** Maya Chen · **Design:** Jonas Weber · **Engineering:** Priya Raman · **Research:** Sam Okafor

## Problem

New teams make a board, look at an empty canvas, and leave. In round-1 interviews, 5 of 8 people stopped at "Bring your agent" because they thought it was required. Only 40% of new workspaces invite a second person in their first week.

## Goals

1. A new team reaches its first shared board in **under 5 minutes**.
2. Week-1 invites go from **40% to 55%** of new workspaces.
3. Drop-off at the agent step is **cut in half**.

## What changes

| Today                             | Onboarding v2                                              |
| --------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| Empty board after sign-up         | Pick a template: Sprint, Design review, Research synthesis |
| Agent step blocks the way forward | Agent step is optional, offered again from the toolbar     |
| Name asked before anything else   | Name asked when you first share                            |
| No example of a good board        | A public sample board, one click from the homepage         |

## Out of scope

- Importing boards from other tools.
- Paid plan prompts during the first session.

## Measuring it

- **Time to first shared board**: from sign-up to a second person opening the board.
- **Invite rate**: share of new workspaces with an invite sent, opened and joined in week 1.
- **Agent step completion**: shown, skipped, completed.
