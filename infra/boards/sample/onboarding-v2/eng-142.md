# ENG-142: Agent step blocks the welcome flow

**Status:** In progress · **Priority:** Urgent · **Assignee:** Priya Raman
**Project:** Onboarding v2 · **Labels:** onboarding, activation

## Problem

New workspaces cannot reach their first board until they finish "Bring your agent". In round-1 interviews, 5 of 8 people stopped at this step; 3 thought Tinkjam would not work without it.

## Acceptance criteria

- Step 2 has a "Skip for now" link that opens the board straight away
- "Bring my agent" stays one click away in the board's toolbar
- Skipping sends `onboarding_agent_skipped`, so the funnel shows the change
